  1. 1The Margin Team view

    Everyone's branch, every Claude session with its state and what it's doing now, your own cards and the team's pull requests. Expand a person to see their agents.

  2. 2CodeLens where it matters

    When a line uses code another teammate is changing, a quiet CodeLens says so: Sam's PR #13 adds useScores, CI passed. Click it for the card. Information, not a warning.

  3. 3The board as an editor tab

    Open board shows the same live board as the big screen, in VS Code's colors. Click a card for its branch, agents, terminals and PR.

  4. 4Your Claude knows the team

    The session starts with a short team snapshot, and Claude reads cards through the MCP server when it needs them. You say "Sam's live scores", it knows the branch, the PR and the function.

  5. 5Status bar

    At a glance: how many teammates and agents are working, and whether someone's agent waits for its human. Click it to open the Margin Team view.

  6. 6Local CI before every push

    When Claude pushes, the repo's CI checks run on your laptop first. A red check holds the push and Claude gets the failure to fix, so GitHub CI only confirms. The board shows local ✓ or push held.
